ABOUT EDSA CUP

More Than a Competition, It's a Celebration of Unity.

EDSA Cup 26 is a sports tournament organized by the English Department Student Association (EDSA), bringing students together through the spirit of sportsmanship, teamwork, and healthy competition.

Featuring Badminton and Futsal, this event provides a platform for students to showcase their talents, challenge their limits, and build meaningful connections beyond the classroom.

Through every match and every moment, we believe that true victory is not only about winning, but also about courage, unity, and the passion to grow together.
